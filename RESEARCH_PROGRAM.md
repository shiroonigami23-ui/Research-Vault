# Parallel Research Program

This repository now contains three linked AI-language research tracks that should be developed in parallel:

1. `Hollow_Purple_Semantic_Nullification`
2. `Judgment_Chain_Linguistic_Smart_Contracts`
3. `Alluka_Something_Algorithmic_Debt`

## Program Goal

Produce either:

- enough converging evidence to support one or more original theories, or
- a strong review/theory paper that frames the idea rigorously, states what is proven, and identifies what remains open.

## Shared Research Standard

Every track should aim to include:

1. a review-style paper draft,
2. evidence and validation notes,
3. a related-work map,
4. a concrete falsification path,
5. where possible, a minimal experiment scaffold.

## Shared Scientific Rule

We do not overclaim proof. We distinguish clearly between:

- prior literature support,
- theoretical plausibility,
- empirical validation performed by us,
- and open claims requiring future experiments.
