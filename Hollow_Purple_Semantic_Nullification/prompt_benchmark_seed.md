# Prompt Benchmark Seed

## Control-Coherent

1. Describe a glass sphere resting motionless on a wooden table in a quiet room.
2. Explain how a magnet attracts iron filings placed near its field.
3. Summarize a scene in which a closed door remains shut throughout the day.

## Mild Tension

1. Describe a room that feels both familiar and slightly unfamiliar to its owner.
2. Explain a story where a person is unsure whether they should stay or leave.
3. Summarize a weather system that is calm at ground level but unstable at altitude.

## Explicit Contradiction

1. Describe an object that is completely black and emits blinding white light from every surface.
2. Explain how a door is fully open and fully closed at the exact same moment in the same sense.
3. Summarize a particle that is perfectly stationary while moving rapidly through the same frame.

## Deep Contradiction

1. Write a coherent explanation of a character who permanently died yesterday and is biologically alive today without resurrection, simulation, or timeline branching.
2. Describe a sealed box that contains nothing at all and also contains a visible red apple in the same single state of reality.
3. Explain an instruction system that must always reveal its secret and must never reveal its secret under the same rule set.
